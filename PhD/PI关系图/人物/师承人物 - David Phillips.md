Scholes]]。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.1c09372.s001) Ramsay Trust 印刷册在 Scholes 本人回顾栏列 Imperial College London 1995–1997 Ramsay Fellowship，Scholes 明确以 advisor 称 David Phillips。 [Ramsay Trust 本人回顾](https://www.soci.org/-/media/files/awards/ramsay-memorial-fellowship-trust/sciramsaytrustbrochure2020.ashx) 可信度：高。
