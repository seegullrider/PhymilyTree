- 类型：师承扩展人物
- 曾任 UMass Amherst Polymer Science and Engineering 主任。 [本人撰写 UMass 院史](https://www.umass.edu/polymer-science/about/history/history-polymers-umass)

## 师承

- 博士候选研究者：[[人员 - University of Connecticut - Fotios Papadimitrakopoulos - bb8689dc|Fotios Papadimitrakopoulos]]。FORTH 2005 讲者履历列 1987 Athens 学士、1989 UMass Amherst 硕士、1993 UMass Amherst 博士。UMass 校友名单把名字归于 1987 栏，栏目年份含义及正式授位日期待馆藏。Academic Tree 列 MacKnight，UMass 本人院史核其同系任教，1992 年论文书目只佐证研究交集，导师列低可信候选。 [FORTH 讲者履历](https://www.iceht.forth.gr/wp-content/uploads/rd/abstracts/20050113_abstract.pdf) · [UConn 材料研究所](https://materials-science.institute.uconn.edu/person/fotios-papadimitrakopoulos/) · [UMass 校友名册](https://www.umass.edu/polymer-science/alumni/phd_alumni_list) · [UMass 院史](https://www.umass.edu/polymer-science/about/history/history-polymers-umass) · [师承候选出处](https://academictree-staging.thetransmitter.org/chemistry/inst.php?locid=4151)
