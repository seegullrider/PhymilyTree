- 博士生：[[人员 - Northwestern - William Halperin - 205f65de|William Halperin]]。[来源](https://studylib.net/doc/11436681/duke-physics-annual-newsletter-2013) 2017 Fritz London 纪念奖得主传记写 Halperin Ph.D. from Cornell University in 1975，并在 graduate studies 阶段写 With his advisor Bob Richardson；Cornell 官方新闻 2013 年称他是 Richardson 的首批学生之一（1969）。以奖项传记的 his advisor 记为博士导师。 [原档 1](https://physics.duke.edu/sites/physics.duke.edu/files/documents/2017LondonHalperin.pdf) · [原档 2](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2013/07/service-honors-statesman-science-robert-richardson)
