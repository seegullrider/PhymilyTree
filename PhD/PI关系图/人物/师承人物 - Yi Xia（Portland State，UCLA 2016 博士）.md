# Yi Xia

> [!info] 师承扩展人物；校属范围待核
> [Portland State 2024 年讲座简介](https://www.pdx.edu/currently/events/physics-seminar-phonon-dynamics-and-heat-transfer-energy-materials)称其自 2022 年任机械与材料工程系教员，研究第一性原理声子、电子输运及材料模拟；该历史介绍不保证现职。Portland State 尚未作为校属记录纳入当前台账。

## 师承

- 博士导师：[[人员 - Yale - Vidvuds Ozolins - 6f9f046d|Vidvuds Ozolins]]。[UCLA 工学院博士典礼册](https://www.seasoasa.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/seasoasa/2017_UCLA-Engineering_Comm_PHD-Names_05_23_2017_Redacted.pdf)列 Yi Xia 2016 材料科学博士、论文《First-Principles Studies of Phonons and Electrons in Bulk Thermoelectrics》及 advisor Vidvuds Ozolins；[Portland State 简介](https://www.pdx.edu/currently/events/physics-seminar-phonon-dynamics-and-heat-transfer-energy-materials)确认博士、年份与研究方向。
- 博士后阶段研究师长：[[人员 - Northwestern - Chris Wolverton - 4472001d|Chris Wolverton]]；[[师承人物 - Maria Chan|Maria Chan]]。[Portland State 简介](https://www.pdx.edu/currently/events/physics-seminar-phonon-dynamics-and-heat-transfer-energy-materials)称其博士后分别与这两位研究者共事，未明确正式 advisor 职衔，具体任期待核。
